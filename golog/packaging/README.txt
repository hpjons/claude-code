GoLog {{VERSION}}
=================

GoLog draws graphic sediment logs. It runs on your own computer, opens in your
web browser and needs no internet connection.

Starting GoLog
--------------
Windows:  Double-click golog.exe. The first time, Windows may say it protected
          your PC: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
macOS:    Double-click golog. The first time, macOS says it can't check GoLog
          for malicious software: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
          scroll down and click "Open Anyway" (macOS 14 and older: right-click
          golog and choose Open).
Linux:    Run ./golog in a terminal.

GoLog opens in your browser. A small window with GoLog's messages stays open
next to it: leave it open while you work.

Stopping GoLog
--------------
Click "Stop GoLog" at the bottom of the Projects page, or press Ctrl+C in
GoLog's window. Everything is saved as you work.

User guide
----------
Click "User guide" on the Projects page.

Your work
---------
Everything is kept in one file, golog.db, in:
  Windows:  %AppData%\GoLog
  macOS:    ~/Library/Application Support/GoLog
  Linux:    ~/.local/share/GoLog
Copy that file to back up your work.
